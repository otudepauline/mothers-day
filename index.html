Dear Mum,

I will not dress this up with an excuse. Yesterday came and went and I did not do what I should have done. But I want you to know that my love for you does not live on a calendar. It lives in every single thing I do.

You are the reason I know what real love looks like. Not the kind that shows up when things are easy, but the kind that stays when things fall apart. You have always been that kind, for me, without fail.

Being your first born is the most important thing I have ever been. Before any achievement, before any title, before anything else, I was yours. And that has shaped everything.

With all the love I have
Your first born, always
